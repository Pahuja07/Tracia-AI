1. CASE BACKGROUND

A Jaipur-based construction company, Arav Infra Projects, reported that ₹8.7 crore had been diverted from several project accounts over an 18-month period.

Initially, investigators believed this was an internal accounting fraud.

Further investigation revealed that the money moved through multiple shell companies, individuals, bank accounts, properties, and intermediaries.

Three apparently unrelated incidents were later connected:
1. ₹8.7 crore diverted from Arav Infra Projects.
2. A property developer was threatened after refusing to transfer a disputed property.
3. A chartered accountant who had discovered suspicious transactions disappeared for several days and later reported intimidation.

Investigators suspect these incidents may be connected to the same criminal network.

E008 - October 2025

E009 - December 2025
Rohit Sharma discovers that several employee email accounts at Arav Infra were accessed from an unusual IP address. One account belongs to Karan Joshi.

E010 - January 2026

E011 - March 2026

E012 - May 2026

8. KEY COMPLICATION

The investigation has multiple possible explanations.

Hypothesis A: Sameer Khan organized the financial fraud internally.
Hypothesis B: Vikram Sethi coordinated the network through legitimate-looking businesses.
Hypothesis C: Deepak Rao operated the shell-company structure.
Hypothesis D: The network consists of several independent actors who collaborated opportunistically.

The available evidence does not automatically establish which hypothesis is correct.

10. AI INVESTIGATION OBJECTIVE

The system can analyze:
- Frequently connected individuals
- Repeated financial relationships
- Unusually central entities
- Common intermediaries
- Communities or clusters
- Indirect connections
- Suspicious transaction chains
- Relationships between seemingly unrelated cases
- Temporal patterns
- Potential investigative leads

Example output:
"P003 has multiple connections to entities appearing in the financial transaction chain and communication records."

The system should not automatically conclude that a person is guilty. It should identify evidence-based investigative leads for human investigators.
